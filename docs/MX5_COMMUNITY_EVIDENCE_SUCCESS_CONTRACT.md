# MX5 — Community Evidence

## Goal

Promote an MX4 Neighborhood to a read-only Community only when retained relationship evidence shows continuing cross-household social connection.

## Required evidence

- at least 2 completed Households in one MX4 Neighborhood;
- at least 1 owner-to-owner social link;
- at least 2 retained relationship evidence records across owners;
- earliest retained cross-owner evidence is at least 60 simulation ticks old.

Physical proximity alone is never sufficient.

## Trade

IC7B physical trade is not released on the MX5 baseline. MX5 therefore reports trade evidence as unavailable/zero and does not invent a trade history.

## Authority

MX5 is shadow-only. No Community state is persisted and no relationship, Household, resource or Settlement state is mutated.

UNKNOWN is never PASS.
